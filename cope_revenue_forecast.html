Forecast Methodology
📐
Seasonal Baseline
Multiplicative seasonal decomposition from FY20–FY24. Monthly indices capture the December/January trough and November/March peaks inherent in COPE's freight cycle.
📈
Exponential Trend
Exponentially weighted year-on-year growth rates with recent years weighted 1.5× prior. Capped at –15%/+20% per year to prevent extrapolation beyond plausible bounds.
🎯
Customer Signal
Four live signals from your Trading Report combine with a 55% dampening factor — a 10% portfolio deterioration moves the forecast ~5.5%, not the full 10%. Adjustments and sundry lines are unaffected.
