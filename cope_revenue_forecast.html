Forecast Methodology
📐
Layer 1
Seasonal Baseline
Multiplicative seasonal decomposition from FY20–FY24. Monthly indices applied to trend-projected annual estimate. Captures Dec/Jan troughs, Nov/Mar peaks.
📈
Layer 1
Exponential Trend
Exponentially weighted YoY growth rates (FY20–FY24), recent years weighted 1.5× prior. Capped at –15%/+20% to prevent extrapolation beyond plausible bounds.
🎯
Layer 2
Customer Signal
4 live signals from your Trading Report (portfolio health, net acquisition, cadence, service mix) combined with Kalman-style dampening: 50% of raw signal applied to revenue lines. Sundry/adjustments unaffected.
